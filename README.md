Problem Statement: The worldwide conversion towards clean energy requires indigenous knowledge of not only 
environmental energy capacity but also public affirmation towards new energy adaptation. This 
following study proposed a combined analytical framework of public posts from different 
platforms and energy capacity data of different states of India with advanced Natural Language 
Processing (NLP) techniques to examine the relationship between public sentiment and clean 
energy adoption across various Indian states. This analysis collects data from 2006 to 2024 to 
bridge the gap between public sentiment and energy capacity due to lack of awareness of the 
potential of clean resources adaptation. 
This investigation uses an integration of traditional methods such as TextBlob 
multidimensionality with advanced Context-Aware Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) to 
capture the multidimensionality of sentiments like environmental or economic impact or safety 
concerns, etc., in a transformer-based DistilBERT model.  
Further, regional disparities are seen in renewable energy capacity. Some states dominate in few 
energy types and lack them in others. The correlation analysis of sentiments and energy capacity 
gives a strong positive relationship (r = 0.84). Hence, these findings underscore the importance 
of integrating public perception into energy planning and provide a data-driven decision-support 
framework for policymakers to identify region-specific renewable energy priorities and promote 
sustainable energy transitions. 
Keywords: Renewable Energy, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Aspect-Based Sentiment 
Analysis, DistilBERT, TextBlob, Pearson Correlation, Public Sentiment, Multi-Platform Data, 
Solar Energy, Wind Energy, India
